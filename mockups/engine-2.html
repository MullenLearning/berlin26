How it’s counted

LOAD · Every run earns points: kilometres weighted by how hard your heart worked. An easy 10 km is about 11 points; the same distance at threshold is about 17.

FITNESS · Your average daily load over the last 42 days. FATIGUE · The same average over the last 7. A hard week spikes fatigue in days; building fitness takes weeks of them.

BASELINES · HRV and resting heart rate use your own 30-night median from Oura as normal, not population numbers.
